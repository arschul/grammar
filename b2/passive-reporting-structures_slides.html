Passive Reporting
Structures

These games are said to increase self-awareness.

It is said that… …is said to…
The idea

Reporting without a source

We use a passive reporting verb to pass on information, opinions or rumours in a formal, cautious way — without saying who said it.

Same idea, two ways

It is said that this behaviour is completely normal.

This behaviour is said to be completely normal.

The structure

1  It + be + past participle + that…

2  Subject + be + past participle + to…

assume · believe · claim · consider · estimate · know · recommend · report · say · suppose · think · understand

Pattern 1

Start with it

An empty subject it, then a full that-clause with its own subject and verb.

It is believed that the ruins are over two thousand years old.
It was reported that nobody had been injured in the fire.
It is estimated that forty per cent of all food is wasted.
Pattern 2

Start with the subject

The subject moves to the front and the verb becomes a to-infinitive.

The ruins are believed to be over two thousand years old.
Two paintings are reported to have been stolen from the gallery.
The company is not thought to be in serious trouble.
Watch the time

Which infinitive?

Pattern 2 has no tense to work with, so the form of the infinitive shows when it happened.

Same time

to + base verb

He is said to live in Lisbon.

She is thought to be working abroad.

Earlier

to have + past participle

The ship is believed to have sunk in 1782.

He is said to have been a spy.

⚠ Watch out

Common mistakes

He is said that he is very wealthy.

Correct: He is said to be very wealthy.

The virus is believed to has spread quickly.

Correct: to have spread quickly.

The team said to be ready for the final.

Correct: The team is said to be ready.

The paper is thought to being much older.

Correct: is thought to be much older.

Practice 2
tap a card to fix it

Spot the mistake

It is said that he to be very rich.

It is said that he is very rich.

✓ After that you need a full clause with a finite verb.

The suspect said to have left the country.

The suspect is said to have left the country.

✓ The reporting verb must be passive: is said.

It was estimate that a million people watched.

It was estimated that a million people watched.

✓ The passive needs the past participle.

The painting is thought to being a forgery.

The painting is thought to be a forgery.

✓ To is followed by the base form, never -ing.
