The idea

Passives without a tense

Most passives carry a tense: was answered, has been told. These two do not. The word in front decides which form you need.

From the interview

They're not all going to be answered by Dr. Davis.

Being asked to listen to my own voice is awful.

The test

cover the passive

look at the word before it

Meaning never decides this. Only the trigger word does.

Trigger 1 · 2 · 3

What comes before?

Three kinds of word, three forms. Nothing else changes.

A modal
be + past participle

Not every question can be answered in one episode.

A to-structure
to be + past participle

I would like to be given the chance to find out more.

Gerund verb / preposition
being + past participle

He doesn't like being introduced to new people.

Form 1

The passive infinitive

After a modal there is no to. After going to, have to, want to and would like to, the to stays.

Listeners should be told in advance. ← modal, no to
They're not all going to be answered by Dr. Davis.
I don't want to be interrupted while I'm recording.
Form 2

The passive gerund

Use being + past participle after gerund verbs and after any preposition. It can also start the sentence.

After a verb

He doesn't like being introduced to new people.

I remember being told about a man with synesthesia.

After a preposition · as subject

She's afraid of being misunderstood.

Being asked to listen to my own voice is awful.

⚠ Watch out

Common mistakes

He doesn't like to be introduced to people.

Correct: he doesn't like being introduced…

The questions must being answered.

Correct: the questions must be answered.

Not all of them will answered today.

Correct: not all of them will be answered.

I'm afraid of be misunderstood.

Correct: I'm afraid of being misunderstood.
