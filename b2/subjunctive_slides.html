The
Subjunctive

It's better that the money go to an organised charity.

that + base verb formal English
The idea

Wanted, not happening

The subjunctive gives weight to requests, orders, demands and recommendations. Its form is the infinitive without to — for every subject.

Examples

It's important that you be comfortable with giving.

I suggest your friend live on the streets for a week.

The structure

verb / adjective + that

+ subject + base verb

That can be left out. The base form stays.

Rule 1

No -s, no tense, always be

The subjunctive ignores the subject and ignores the tense of the main verb.

he goes → that he go  ·  she arrives → that she arrive
I am / you are / he is → that I be, that you be, that he be
The committee recommended that the rule be changed. (not was changed)
Rule 2

What triggers it

Verbs

ask · demand · insist · propose · recommend · request · require · suggest · urge

The doctor insisted that she stay in bed.

Adjectives

essential · good · better · important · necessary · vital · crucial

It is vital that every passenger have a ticket.

Rule 3

Negatives & alternatives

To make it negative, put not straight before the base verb. There is no do and no doesn't.

We insist that he not be told about the surprise.
The court ordered that she not leave the country.
British English also allows should + base verb: I suggest that he should go.
⚠ Watch out

Common mistakes

I recommend that she to take the earlier train.

Correct: that she take the earlier train.

It's better that the money goes to charity.

Correct: that the money go to charity.

They recommended that the rule was changed.

Correct: that the rule be changed.

We insist that he be not told.

Correct: that he not be told.

Spot the mistake

We ask that all visitors to sign in at reception.

We ask that all visitors sign in at reception.

✓ The subjunctive is the infinitive without to.

The committee recommended that the rule was changed.

The committee recommended that the rule be changed.

✓ The subjunctive never changes for tense.

We insist that he be not told about the surprise.

We insist that he not be told about the surprise.

✓ Not goes directly before the base verb.

It is better that the money given to charity.

It is better that the money be given to charity.

✓ A passive subjunctive still needs be.
