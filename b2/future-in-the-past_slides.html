Future in
the Past

I was going to start my project last night, but I decided to wait.

past forms future meaning
The idea

Standing in the past, looking forward

Plans, intentions and obligations that were still in the future when somebody spoke or thought about them — but that moment has passed.

Examples

The school was filming her lecture later that day.

We were supposed to read the chapter before class.

The rule

future idea

+ past form

Never will, never am going to — everything shifts back.

Forms 1 & 2

Schedules and reporting

past progressive for a scheduled activity  ·  would + base verb for what was said

The school was filming her lecture later that day.
We arrived early because our train was leaving at six.
I told the team I would be at the meeting.

"I will call you." → He said he would call me.

Forms 3 & 4

Plans and obligations

was / were going to = an intention  ·  was / were supposed to = an obligation

I was going to start last night, but I decided to wait.
We were supposed to read it online, but there were problems.
She was supposed to hand it in on Monday, but she forgot.

Always to + base verb: going to start, not going start.

The meaning

Did it actually happen?

neutral
was filming · would be

The school was filming her lecture later.

It may or may not have happened.

it didn't happen
was going to · was supposed to

I was going to call you, but I ran out of battery.

The plan was dropped or the duty unmet.

If nothing went wrong, use a simple past instead: I called you yesterday.

⚠ Watch out

Common mistakes

I told them I will be there.

Correct: I told them I would be there.

I was going start the report.

Correct: I was going to start the report.

We were supposed read the chapter.

Correct: We were supposed to read the chapter.

He said he would to come.

Correct: He said he would come.

Spot the mistake

I told the team I will be at the meeting.

I told the team I would be at the meeting.

✓ After a past reporting verb, will becomes would.

I was going start my project last night.

I was going to start my project last night.

✓ Going needs to before the base verb.

We were supposed read the whole chapter.

We were supposed to read the whole chapter.

✓ Supposed is always followed by to + base verb.

I was supposed to went to the dentist yesterday.

I was supposed to go to the dentist yesterday.

✓ Base verb after to — the past is already in was.
