What is happening right now?

Things happening right now

We use the present continuous for actions in progress at this moment.

I am working right now.

She is reading a book.

They are playing football.

The structure

subject
+ am / is / are
+ verb‑ing

Two parts, every time. Never drop am, is or are.

Making the -ing form

Most verbsjust add -ing
play→playing
read→reading
wait→waiting
Ends in silent -edrop the e, add -ing
write→writing
make→making
dance→dancing
Short verb: vowel + consonantdouble the last letter
sit→sitting
run→running
swim→swimming

Look! Listen! Right now!

These words often tell us something is happening at this moment.

Look! The dog is chasing the cat in the garden.
Right now, my sister is doing her homework.
Can you be quiet? I am trying to study.
Listen! Someone is knocking on the door.

Common mistakes

✗I ^ cooking dinner.

✓I am cooking dinner.

✗The dogs is barking.

✓The dogs are barking.

✗She is writeing a letter.

✓She is writing a letter.

✗She not is reading.

✓She isn't reading.
