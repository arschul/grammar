Phrasal Verbs:
Separable & Non-Separable

I looked it up. — not "looked up it".

separable non-separable
The two types

Can the object go in the middle?

Separable
Noun can go before or after the particle.

I looked up "body language". ✓
I looked "body language" up. ✓

Non-separable
Object always follows the full phrasal verb — nothing in between.

You can rely on Ruby. ✓
You can rely Ruby on. ✗

💡 The pronoun rule

Pronouns must go in the middle

✓ I looked it up. (pronoun between verb and particle)
✗ I looked up it. (pronoun after particle — never correct)
✓ She was making it up.  |  I told him you would call him back.
Separable phrasal verbs

Common ones to know

look upmake upcall back take upturn offput on pick upwrite down
Turn off the light.  =  Turn the light off.  =  Turn it off.
Pick up the kids.  =  Pick the kids up.  =  Pick them up.
Non-separable phrasal verbs

Object always follows the full verb

rely onrun intolook after come up withget on withdeal with
✓ I ran into Alice outside class.
✓ She came up with a great idea.    ✗ She came up a great idea with.
⚠ Watch out

Common mistakes

Please turn off it.

Please turn it off. (pronoun must split)

I can rely her on.

I can rely on her. (non-sep)

She came up a plan with.

She came up with a plan.

Can you pick up it?

Can you pick it up?
