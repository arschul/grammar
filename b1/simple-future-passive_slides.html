Simple Future
Passive

Clothes will be made smarter — and sneakers won't be made of leather.

will be + participle predictions
The idea

The thing, not the doer

We use it for what we think will or won't happen. Usually we don't say who does the action — we don't know, or it isn't important.

Compare

Somebody will build solar panels. → focus on an unknown somebody

Solar panels will be built. → focus on the panels

The structure

will + be

+ past participle

be never changes — singular or plural, any subject.

Form

Positive, negative, question

will be + participle  ·  won't be + participle  ·  Will + subject + be + participle?

More eco-friendly fabrics will be used.
The prices for high-end fabrics won't be reduced.
Will the results be published tomorrow?

The house will be sold. The houses will be sold. — be never moves.

Changing the focus

From active to passive

The object of the active sentence becomes the subject of the passive one.

They will build solar panels.  →  Solar panels will be built.
A local firm will design the logo.  →  The logo will be designed by a local firm.
They won't announce the winner today.  →  The winner won't be announced today.

Going to works the same way: The old system is going to be replaced.

Get this right

The past participle

make → made
build → built

Clothes will be made smarter.

sell → sold
take → taken

The tickets will be sold online.

write → written
choose → chosen

The winners will be chosen in May.

grow → grown
send → sent

More food will be grown locally.

⚠ Watch out

Common mistakes

The tickets will sold online.

Correct: The tickets will be sold online.

Clothes will be make from cotton.

Correct: Clothes will be made from cotton.

The winners will be choosed.

Correct: The winners will be chosen.

The prices won't be not reduced.

Correct: The prices won't be reduced.
